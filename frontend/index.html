How the 3D ULPIN Works

A standard 2D ULPIN identifies a ground survey plot. Our 3D ULPIN extends it with a floor and unit suffix, so every flat/unit in a multi-storey building gets its own unique, linked identifier.

KA-BLR-00042-F05-U03
Base ULPIN — state, district, survey plot (unchanged from 2D cadastre)
Floor — F01-F05, ground up
Unit — U01-U04 within that floor
